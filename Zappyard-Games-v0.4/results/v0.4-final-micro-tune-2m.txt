Zappyard Slot 01 — v0.4 final micro-tune

Design objective
- Keep the game engaging at ordinary stake-session scale.
- Preserve the existing ~33% hit frequency and small/medium-win cadence.
- Ensure target RTP is at least ~96%.
- Concentrate the incremental return in rare and super-rare outcomes rather than increasing routine wins.

Math change from v0.3
- 5–15 symbol cluster payouts: UNCHANGED.
- 16+ symbol cluster payout bands: 6× their v0.3 values.
- Symbol weights: unchanged.
- BONUS frequency/rules: unchanged.
- Tile multiplier growth: unchanged.
- Base powerup odds: unchanged.
- Free Spin powerup multiplier: unchanged at 2.5×.
- Added explicit 50,000× max-win constant for the production ruleset.

Why this distribution fits the target
- The hit rate remains ~33.2%, so the game does not become materially deader.
- The entire 5–15 cluster pay structure is untouched, preserving the small/medium reward loop.
- The additional RTP is paid primarily through very large clusters, which are intrinsically rare.
- 500×+ occurred about once per 32,258 paid spins in this sample.
- 1,000×+ occurred about once per 111,111 paid spins.
- 5,000×+ occurred once in 2,000,000 paid spins.
- The theoretical ceiling remains 50,000×.

Important statistical note
Monte Carlo RTP is an estimate, not a mathematical guarantee. Because v0.4 deliberately puts more return into rare outcomes, measured RTP will fluctuate more between finite simulation runs. The 96.43588% figure is the current 2M-spin estimate; a much larger certification-style simulation and Stake Engine validation are still required before treating RTP as final/declared.
